## 2. Precisely one causal question

Does `CE+KL(q_parent || p_student)` with **fixed lambda=1.0, temperature=1.0**, on the exact frozen ACT parent and linear Decision Head, reduce Correction-specific harm to previously untrained sibling decisions compared with CE **without an unacceptable loss of Correction Repair** when evaluated on *new, prospectively frozen family-level pairs* that differ structurally from the previously used **key-only sibling-pair generator**?

Keep **one** manipulated factor: training objective. The new family distribution is applied identically to both objective conditions; no λ search, architecture or tokenizer change.

### What prospective independence would mean

1. Every proposed family's `(operation,left,right,store_as)`, sibling/correction task ID, and leakage group must be disjoint from the prior 30 families, dev probe, DIAG members, and known non-sealed baseline train/cal/dev identifiers. Do not read/open the project sealed test to accomplish this.
2. **Change the pair-generating rule, not just IDs or numeric literals**: a candidate *design* is a predeclared `numeric_operand_perturbation + new_key` sibling relationship rather than key-only. Sibling and correction use the same operation (add or subtract) but their operand pair is intentionally different according to a committed deterministic transformation; both `store_as` keys differ. Both task roles use existing `calculate_and_store` syntax, CandidateBuilder, RuleBasedPolicy and TaskEvaluator—no code-path modification is allowed under the single-factor comparison.
3. Balance candidate operation strata (`add`, `subtract`), predeclare the operand sampling domain and transformation, family/source seeds, exclusion/deduplication policy and **full ordered explicit task manifest** before scoring a parent or corrected student on any new evaluation case. Never select families based on whether KL helps.
4. Distinct arithmetic tuples give *conditional independent task samples* under a fixed generator if honestly drawn; they do **not** automatically give independent **generator templates**. A new sibling-pair rule supports a **narrow replication** to operand+key perturbations, not broad autonomous agent generalization. If full generator-template independence is required, return to the design gate and build a distinct supported task family in a separate explicitly approved project; do not call renames OOD.

### Proposed prospective enrollment (NOT frozen)

- **Planning candidate:** 30 independently selected family groups, balanced 15 add / 15 subtract, each with exactly one correction and one distinct untrained sibling, one fixed training seed (`42`) per family. This is a CPU budget proposal, **not** selection of families.
- Objective `CE` versus `CE+parentKL` × Replay/Correction, each for 100 AdamW steps at LR `0.003`; only head trainable, frozen backbone, checkpoints `0,10,25,50,100`, `2` CPU threads. Candidate 30×2×2 = **120 training trajectories** and **600 arm-checkpoint observations**, 12,000 training steps across arms, roughly **7.5×** DIAG v5's trajectory count, before any measured runtime estimate; confirm budget in WSL *without opening endpoints*.
- Parent artifact/run, vocab/tokenizer digests, candidate serializer `aster-decision-input-0`, `calculate-and-store-v0` action builder, parent-Q detach, rollout policy `model_only`, candidate ordering, base supervision and Replay matching all anchored exactly to DIAG v5. Do not treat seed or checkpoint as an independent family.
- A different task-pair generator potentially causes correction-floor effects. Candidate coverage and RuleBased teacher trajectory existence may be inspected pre-outcome, but **model-scored eligibility, intervention success or sibling margin must not be used to select families**.
- If zero interventions occur in a prospectively selected family, report a predeclared not-trainable/failure outcome; do not silently drop/reseed it or backfill a favorable family. Abort the study if the underlying candidate/evaluator protocol fails structurally and report the audit failure without inspecting primary outcomes.

## 3. Endpoints, evidence boundaries and proposed analysis

For fixed family `f` and objective `o`:

```
D(f,o) = [marginSibling(Correction,100)-marginSibling(Correction,0)]
       - [marginSibling(Replay,100)-marginSibling(Replay,0)]

g(f) = D(f,CE+KL) - D(f,CE)

Repair_CR(f,o) = [repairAcc(Correction,100)-repairAcc(Correction,0)]
               - [repairAcc(Replay,100)-repairAcc(Replay,0)]

r(f) = Repair_CR(f,CE+KL)-Repair_CR(f,CE)
```

Primary proposed family-level response `g(f)` (higher = less interference). Required distinct Repair preservation co-criterion `r(f)` relative to a **prospectively defensible non-inferiority bound**. Supplementary: absolute sign and scale of `D(f,CE+KL)`, sibling accuracy, base retention, episode success, candidate coverage, CE/KL components, parameter drift, resource budget. Never promote a secondary endpoint after looking at results.

**Power sensitivity from Wolfram, with no ties and independent Bernoulli family wins, one-sided alpha 0.05:** 30 families require **at least 20 wins** to reject a 50% win-rate null (`P_0(X>=20)=0.0493686`); when the underlying win probability is 0.70, the corresponding power is only **0.73037**. This calculation addresses a **sign-probability claim only**, not a positive average margin or Repair non-inferiority. Ties, magnitude thresholds, shared generator correlations, or ineligible families can reduce usable evidence. Avoid a post-hoc epsilon tuned to small DIAG v5 differences.

**Non-inferiority is not solved.** Because `r(f)` is a difference of paired accuracy contrasts, its conservative bound is `[-2,+2]`; a one-sided 95% distribution-free Hoeffding bound for the family mean with `n=30` has a half-width of approximately **0.894**, too large to defend a small NI margin such as 0.05. A narrower paired confidence method requires explicit variance, coverage assumptions, clinically/task-relevant margin justification and a prospective power calculation; otherwise do not call the joint result confirmatory. Proposed outcome label until resolved: **`prospective_reproducibility_exploratory`**, even if a sign-test p-value appears small.

**Do not freeze the above sample size or analysis yet.** Before a genuine preregistered outcome-bearing run, pin (a) endpoint scale and minimum material change, (b) number of families/seeds and sample-size rationale, (c) null, one-/two-sided test and ties policy, (d) repair non-inferiority δ and confidence interval method, (e) multiplicity/joint decision logic, (f) missingness and exception handling. If the financial/CPU budget cannot support inferential NI, restrict the research claim explicitly to prospective exploratory replication.
